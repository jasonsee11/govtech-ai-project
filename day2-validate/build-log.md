# Build Log

**Scenario:** C · MOE Lower Secondary Biology

## Riskiest Assumption

Teachers will trust the accuracy of AI-generated assessment questions enough to use them, rather than starting from scratch.

## Risk Type

Value — will teachers find the generated content trustworthy and useful enough to change their existing workflow?

## Kill Switch

Stop if teachers consistently reject or heavily rewrite more than 50% of generated questions. This signals that the AI output is not a better starting point than their current resources.

## Success Signal

At least 7 in 10 teachers retain the generated questions with minor or no edits during prototype testing.

## What We're Testing

The prototype includes:
- Generated questions with curriculum learning outcome references
- One question flagged as "uncertain alignment" to test if teachers notice and respond to uncertainty signals
- One subtly incorrect question (made up, labelled) to test if teachers catch errors
- Keep / Edit / Reject buttons to measure retention rate
