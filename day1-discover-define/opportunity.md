# Opportunity: Enable lower secondary science teachers to create high-quality, curriculum-aligned biology assessments in under two hours, giving them more time to focus on teaching and learning.
**Scenario:** C · MOE lower secondary biology
All quotes and metrics are synthetic and labelled as made up.
## Problem Framework
### 1. Goal
Enable lower secondary science teachers to create high-quality, accurate and curriculum-aligned biology assessments in under two hours, giving them more time to focus on teaching and learning.
### 2. Problem
Teachers understand the direction of the revised biology curriculum but struggle to translate it into practical assessments within their existing workload.
They spend considerable time:
Interpreting curriculum learning outcomes and content boundaries.
Searching for and adapting existing assessment resources.
Designing questions with appropriate difficulty and cognitive demand.
Developing answers and marking schemes.
Checking scientific accuracy and curriculum alignment.
Teachers currently spend an average of 5.5 hours creating one aligned biology assessment, against a target of under two hours.
### 3. Context
MOE is rolling out a revised lower secondary biology curriculum across 140 secondary schools, involving approximately 1,100 science teachers and 40,000 students.
Schools must be ready within 12 weeks. Only 41% of schools currently feel ready, against a target of 90%.
Teachers are preparing for the revised curriculum alongside their existing teaching, marking and administrative responsibilities. Heads of Department are responsible for supporting teachers and ensuring the quality and consistency of curriculum implementation.
### 4. Constraints
Student data and assessment integrity must be protected.
Assessment content must be grounded in the approved curriculum and relevant science strand.
Scientific accuracy and pedagogical quality must not be compromised for efficiency.
Teachers retain professional judgement and accountability for all assessment content used with students.
Teachers will trust a new tool only if their first experience is good.
Use synthetic data and non-confidential assessment materials for prototyping.
### 5. Success
Pass if at least 7 in 10 teachers can produce an accurate, curriculum-aligned formative assessment in under two hours, with only minor revisions to the generated content. Fail if below 5 in 10.
Additional quality indicators:
At least 90% of generated questions are correctly aligned with the intended learning outcomes. 
Zero critical inaccuracies in teacher-approved assessments. 
At least 80% of participating teachers are willing to use the tool again.
### 6. Explore
Will teachers trust and refine AI-generated assessments or prefer to create their own from scratch?
Does showing the curriculum learning outcome and source for each question increase teacher confidence?
How much control do teachers need over question types, difficulty and cognitive demand?
Does the tool genuinely reduce workload, or simply shift the effort from creation to verification?
Would teachers offload this?
